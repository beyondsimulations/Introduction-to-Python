The city just banned deliveries after 22:00, so your app has to learn to say
"sorry, we're closed" at the right moment. In this notebook you teach it to make
decisions with `if` / `elif` / `else`, apply a discount ladder to bigger orders,
and loop over the day's orders to total them up, while fixing Kevin's off-by-one
order counter along the way. It ends in a price war with MunchCorp that only a
`while` loop can win.
